# Tripartite

**An independent venture studio building ideas into technology-driven ventures.**

Tripartite brings together people, technology and ambitious ideas to create useful products and new businesses. We take projects from an early concept through research and prototyping to a product ready to grow as a standalone venture.

## How we work

- **Find the opportunity** — start with a real need and a clear problem to solve.
- **Make it tangible** — test ideas quickly with thoughtful design and working prototypes.
- **Build for the long term** — develop the product, technology and foundations needed to launch and grow.

Our projects bring together product thinking and software engineering, with a focus on turning promising ideas into practical, lasting products.
